Egg-Drop Soup

Description

That warm fuzzy feeling when you take your first sip of Egg-Drop Soup at a Chinese restaurant can't be beat, unless you bring the restaurant to you. Our easy Egg-Drop Soup recipe is just what you need to make a good soup without the restaurant price. With its rich blend of flavors and its beautiful presentation of egg flowers (made by you), you will have a hard time keeping yourself from coming back for more. Let's jump right in and see how easy it is to make this recipe from home.

Ingredients list

  • 4 cups chicken stock
  • 1/2 tsp sesame oil
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 1/8 tsp sugar
  • 1/8 tsp white pepper
  • 1/2 tsp tumeric
  • 3 Tbsp. corn starch
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 scallion
  • 1/4 tsp MSG

Steps

  1. Bring chicken stock to a boil and add sesame oil, salt, sugar, MSG, and white pepper.
  2. Add in tumeric and stir, this gives the soup its signature yellow color.
  3. Taking the corn starch, mix into 1/3 cups of water and stir until disolved. Add this mixture to the soup.
  4. Continuously stir and add more or less cornstarch depending on preferred thickness.
  5. In a bowl, lightly beat the eggs.
  6. Using a ladel, continuously stir the soup while pouring in the lightly beaten eggs. Admire your beautifully formed egg flowers!
  7. Serve soup with a ladel and top with scallion if desired.
