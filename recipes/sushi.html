Basic California Roll

Description

Ingredients List

  • 3 1/2 ounces crab sticks, finely chopped
  • 4 Tbsp. mayonnaise
  • 1/2 cucumber
  • 4 sheets of nori (dried seaweed)
  • 2 1/2 cups cooked sushi rice
  • 2 1/2 Tbsp. sesame seeds
  • 1 avacado, sliced

Steps

  1. In a bowl mix the finely chopped crab with the mayonnaise
  2. Next, thinly slice the cucumbers into spears
  3. Lay your nori sheets down on a flat surface, with wet fingers pat the cooked sushi rice ontop of the nori.
  4. Sprinkle the rice with sesame seeds evenly and then carefully flip the nori sheet upside down.
  5. Place 1 Tbsp. of crab mixture into a line and place a few cucumber spears on top, add 1 to 2 avacado slices.
  6. Using a bamboo mat or siran wrap, roll the sushi roll up into a cyclinder.
  7. Slice the sushi roll into 3/4-inches.
  8. Serve with ginger, soy sauce, and wasabi. Enjoy!
