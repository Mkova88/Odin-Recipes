Asian Cucumber Salad

Description

This Asian Cucumber Salad will transform your taste buds from the first bite! This salad channels the orient with its diverse flavors, its crisp and refreshing feel, and of course a good kick of heat. You'll be coming back for seconds. With this easy to follow recipe you will be making your new favorite salad in no time at all. A few simple ingredients and minimal prep time is all that is required so lets jump right in!

Ingredients

  • 1 large english cucumber
  • 1/8 tsp. salt
    • For the dressing
  • 3/4 Tbsp. sugar
  • 1/2 Tbsp. soy sauce
  • 1/2 Tbsp. rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp. sesame seeds, toasted
  • 1/2 to 1 tsp. red pepper flakes
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder

Steps

  1. Place cucumber on a cutting board and smash it lightly along its full length using a meat mallet until it cracks lightly.
  2. Cut into 1/2 inch peices, move to a mixing bowl and add salt, stir until combined
  3. Add the rest of the dressing ingredients to the bowl and toss.
  4. Taste the sauce and adjust any of the ingridents to preference
  5. Serve immediately. This salad is best eaten with a spoon, scooping up some of the dressing with each bite!
