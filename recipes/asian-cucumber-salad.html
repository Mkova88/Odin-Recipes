Asian Cucumber Salad

Description

This Asian Cucumber Salad will transform your taste buds from the first bite! This salad channels the orient with its diverse flavors, its crisp and refreshing feel, and of course a good kick of heat, you'll be coming back for more. With this easy to follow recipe you will be making your new favorite salad in no time at all. A few simple ingredients and minimal prep time is all that is required so lets jump right in!

Steps

  • 1 large english cucumber
  • 1/8 tsp. salt

    • For the dressing

  • 3/4 Tbsp. sugar
  • 1/2 Tbsp. soy sauce
  • 1/2 Tbsp. rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp. sesame seeds, toasted
  • 1/2 to 1 tsp. red pepper flakes
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder
